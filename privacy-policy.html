Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026

523 Studio Creative Dashboard is an internal dashboard used for social media content analytics and content strategy.

Information We Access

With the user's authorization, the application may access TikTok profile information, public video information, and available performance metrics through the TikTok API.

How We Use Information

The information is used only for content performance analysis, reporting, and generating data-driven content strategy recommendations.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or share user data with third parties for advertising or unrelated purposes.

Data Access and Removal

Users may revoke access to their TikTok account at any time. Stored integration data may also be removed upon request.

Contact

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact 523 Studio.
